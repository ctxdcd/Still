A NOTE TO MYSELF

Why I made STILL.

STILL. is a personal collection of principles I want to remember when my thoughts become overwhelming. I made this space to remind myself that I don't have to figure everything out at once, that not every thought deserves my attention, and that I can only work with what is within my reach. These are not rules for becoming a perfect person. They are reminders to return to when I lose perspective.
